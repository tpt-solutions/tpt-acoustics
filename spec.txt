tpt-acoustics Design Specification (spec.txt)
1. Overview
Name: tpt-acoustics
Description: A pure-Rust, AI-native computational acoustics library. It provides geometric room acoustics, wave acoustics (BEM/FEM for the Helmholtz equation), aeroacoustics, vibroacoustics, and psychoacoustic modeling without C/C++ FFI or external solver dependencies.
License: MIT (all dependencies must be MIT or compatible; no Apache-2.0-only crates).
Core Philosophy: Unit-safe, no_std + alloc compatible at the core, differentiable for neural acoustic rendering and inverse design, and mathematically rigorous via native Rust verification tooling and from-scratch implementations.
2. Design Principles
Zero External Solver Dependencies: No COMSOL FFI, no ODEON, no C++ acoustic engines. All algorithms (Image Source Method, Boundary Element Method for Helmholtz, Ffowcs Williams-Hawkings) are implemented from scratch in pure Rust, leveraging tpt-math for linear algebra and tpt-fem for structural coupling.
Strict Licensing: No Apache-2.0-only dependencies. The workspace strictly uses MIT, MIT/Apache-2.0 dual, or custom in-house crates.
Type-State & Dimensional Safety: Compile-time prevention of unit mismatches. Phantom types distinguish between acoustic pressure (Pascals), particle velocity (m/s), and power (Watts). Sound pressure levels (SPL) are strictly handled in logarithmic decibel scales with proper reference pressures.
Native Formal Verification: Safety and physical invariants enforced via:
Kani Rust Verifier: For bounded model checking of critical numerical loops (e.g., proving the Image Source Method doesn't exceed maximum reflection orders, or verifying CFL condition stability in time-domain wave solvers).
Property-Based Testing (proptest): For statistical verification of physical laws (e.g., "reciprocity holds in acoustic transfer functions", "energy is conserved in lossless ray tracing").

tpt-acou-ray
Purpose: High-frequency geometric acoustics for room modeling and auralization.
Key Features:
Image Source Method (ISM): Exact analytical calculation of early reflections for convex polyhedral rooms.
Ray Tracing: Monte Carlo ray tracing for late reverberation, utilizing tpt-percept's spatial indexing (k-d trees/BVH) for fast scene intersection.
Frequency-dependent absorption and scattering coefficients at boundaries.
Differentiable ray tracing hooks for inverse room geometry estimation.
tpt-acou-wave
Purpose: Low-to-mid frequency wave acoustics using numerical PDE solvers.
Key Features:
From-scratch Boundary Element Method (BEM) solver for the exterior/interior Helmholtz equation.
Dense and hierarchical (Fast Multipole Method - FMM) matrix assembly for large-scale BEM.
Integration with tpt-fem for interior acoustic cavity modes (coupled acoustic-structural eigenvalue problems).
tpt-acou-vibro
Purpose: Coupling between structural vibration and acoustic radiation.
Key Features:
Calculation of radiation efficiency and acoustic transfer vectors.
Forced response of plates/shells to acoustic excitation (Transmission Loss, Sound Transmission Class - STC).
Directly consumes mode shapes and frequency response functions (FRFs) from tpt-fem-modal.
tpt-acou-aero
Purpose: Prediction of noise generated by fluid flow (aeroacoustics).
Key Features:
Acoustic analogy implementations: Lighthill's equation, Curle's extension for solid boundaries.
Ffowcs Williams-Hawkings (FW-H) equation solver for rotating machinery (fans, rotors, propellers).
Integration with tpt-physics (CFD) to extract turbulent flow source terms (Lighthill stress tensor).
tpt-acou-psycho
Purpose: Modeling human perception of sound for audio engineering and VR/AR.
Key Features:
Standard weighting filters (A, B, C, ITU-R 468).
Spatial audio: Head-Related Transfer Functions (HRTFs), Ambisonics (B-format) encoding/decoding, and VBAP (Vector Base Amplitude Panning).
Loudness models (Zwicker, Moore) and masking curves.

Purpose: Mathematical verification of acoustic algorithms.
Key Features:
proptest strategies for generating valid room geometries and impedance boundary conditions.
Kani harnesses to prove the Image Source Method correctly handles edge cases (e.g., grazing angles, non-convex room validation).

5. Integration & Synergies
tpt-fem: Provides the structural vibration modes and harmonic response. tpt-acou-vibro uses these to compute acoustic radiation. tpt-acou-wave uses tpt-fem-sparse for interior acoustic FEM.
tpt-percept: tpt-acou-ray leverages tpt-percept-cloud's spatial indexing (BVH/Octrees) for high-performance ray-scene intersection in complex 3D environments.
tpt-dsp: Provides the FFTs and digital filters used by tpt-acou-psycho (weighting filters) and for converting time-domain aeroacoustic pressure signals into frequency spectra.
tpt-physics: Provides the unsteady CFD flow fields required as source terms for tpt-acou-aero (FW-H solver).
AI-Native Agents: Differentiable acoustic transfer functions allow tpt-eve / tpt-anima agents to perform neural acoustic rendering, optimize concert hall geometries via gradient descent, or train spatial audio models.
